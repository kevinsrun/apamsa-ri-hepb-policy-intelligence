Educational serology patterns

CDC 2023 recommends initial HBsAg, anti-HBs and total anti-HBc screening for adults. This tool describes typical patterns, not a diagnosis. Negative IgM, recent negative HBsAg or ALT >10× ULN alone do not establish chronicity.
