Verified Public Health Benchmarks

  • CDC Universal Adult Screening Mandate: In March 2023, the CDC established universal screening for all adults aged ≥18 at least once in their lifetime using the complete triple panel (HBsAg, anti-HBs, total anti-HBc).
  • Rhode Island Infection Burden: In the CDC Viral Hepatitis Surveillance Report (Table 2.3), Rhode Island reported 364 newly identified chronic hepatitis B cases (rate of 33.3/100,000 population)—ranking among the highest state rates in the nation.
  • Perinatal Prevention Mandate: RIDOH operates a strict universal birth-dose policy under R.I. Code R. 14-040-002, requiring positive maternal HBsAg reporting within 4 working days and newborn vaccination within 24 hours.
  • Urban Immigrant Concentration: Providence County accounts for 60.3% of the state's population and over 80% of foreign-born residents (20.8% foreign-born, 6.24% Asian American).

Methodological Boundaries

Key Analytical Boundaries:

• Aggregate claims cannot measure individual triple-panel completion.
• Absence of physician billing does not indicate clinical noncompliance (commercial reference labs bill globally).
• Zero Personal Health Information (PHI) is ingested or exposed.
