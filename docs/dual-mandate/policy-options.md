# Preliminary policy options memorandum — internal draft

To: Brown APAMSA hepatitis B initiative research team  
Status: evidence development; no outreach, submission or external publication authorized.

1. **Make measurement reliable before asserting a screening gap.** Request aggregate, disclosure-controlled HBV surveillance and perinatal cohort definitions from RIDOH through an approved process. Specify data year, incident versus newly reported chronic definitions, case completeness and eligible pregnancy denominator. Success measure: reproducible numerator/denominator pairs with a documented release rule. Do not invent a county allocation if disclosure limits prohibit one.
2. **Pilot a guideline-aligned workflow with willing community partners.** The [CDC adult screening recommendation](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/72/rr/rr7201a1.htm) supplies a clinical rationale; local patient-level implementation is not measured here. A possible future pilot would track unique eligible adults, offered tests, completed panels, returned results and completed linkage visits, with consent, privacy review, language access and clinician leadership. These are proposed evaluation measures, not observed outcomes or an approved clinical protocol.
3. **Audit coverage and navigation before proposing a mandate.** Compare [Medicare NCD 210.6](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncd.aspx?ncdid=369), RI Medicaid materials and actual partner billing experience. Investigate whether administrative eligibility changes disrupt longitudinal HBV care, without assuming an HBV-specific effect from general coverage notices. Identify a responsible navigator and escalation process. No patient eligibility advice or count of disenrolled HBV patients can be inferred from the current data.
4. **Use occupational and perinatal programs as defined implementation settings.** Review the active tattoo rule and perinatal program with responsible agencies after outreach approval. Possible measures include documented vaccine-offer policy compliance or complete infant follow-up, with valid denominators. Do not conflate occupational vaccination, maternal screening and adult universal screening.
5. **Keep legislative monitoring session-specific.** Verify S0751's final disposition and search official current-session records before drafting any legislative recommendation. Distinguish historical awareness resolutions, existing law, proposed amendments and new policy options. Legal/clinical review and an operator decision precede any stakeholder-facing proposal.

Selection should consider feasibility, community input, privacy, language access, follow-up capacity and measurable benefit. No option is ranked by the unsupported county need scores or prototype procedure ratios. Cost-effectiveness, budgets and anticipated case yields remain unestimated pending credible local inputs.
